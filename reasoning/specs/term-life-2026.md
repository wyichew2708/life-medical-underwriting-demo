# ProtectMax Term Life 2026 — Product Specification Sheet

Fictional product written for this demonstration. Not a real product and not an offer.

Product ID: protectmax-term-2026
Product Name: ProtectMax Term Life 2026
Product Type: Life
Currency: SGD

## Eligibility

Minimum issue age: 18 years
Maximum entry age: 65 years
Minimum sum assured: SGD 50,000
Maximum sum assured: SGD 3,000,000
Minimum policy term: 5 years
Maximum policy term: 40 years

## Underwriting limits

Non-medical limit: SGD 750,000
Automatic referral limit: SGD 2,500,000
Smoker definition: 12 months tobacco or nicotine free
Maximum BMI: 38

## Required evidence

- Completed health declaration for all applications
- Paramedical examination and blood profile above the non-medical limit
- Attending physician statement where a condition is declared
- Financial questionnaire above SGD 2,500,000

## Exclusions

- Suicide within 12 months of issue or reinstatement
- Death arising from war or active participation in civil commotion
- Death while engaged in professional motorsport or competitive diving
- Death arising from a pre-existing condition not disclosed at application

## Benefits

- Level term death benefit
- Terminal illness advance of the death benefit
- Optional critical illness rider, underwritten separately
