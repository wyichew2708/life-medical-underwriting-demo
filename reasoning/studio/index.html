4 · Evaluate and tune

Evaluation compares every case with its recorded decision. Tuning searches the bounded surface — rule thresholds inside declared bounds, retrieval, the prompt's presentation variant, and with a model configured, guidance lines the model proposes from its own misses — cross-validated, and applies a round only when held-out cases confirm it and less-cautious disagreements do not rise.
